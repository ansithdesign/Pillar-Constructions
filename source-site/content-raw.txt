PILLAR EXTERIOR PANEL
The Pillar Exterior Panel (EP), is a factory-fabricated exterior wall assembly comprised
of the cold-formed metal-framing, exterior sheathing, and air-vapor barrier (AVB). It
requires no changes to the project’s specifications, and can be assembled utilizing any
of the well known brands in the industry.

RAINSCREEN SYSTEMS
Pillar knows Rainscreen Systems!
Advancement in building technologies and the invention of new cladding products bring versatile solutions to the building skin challenge.
Exterior claddings, wall protection products, exterior substructures and supporting framing all behave as a synergetic assembly.
Our experience with all components of the building skin provides a comprehensive approach to the building's envelope.
Pillar Construction understands and manages all the components of the building envelope for a seamless installation with a more controlled
quality and sequencing of components.
We utilize 3D scanning to account for as-built field conditions allowing out final product to appear flawless and to conceal substrate irregularities.
We pride ourselves in our ability to work together with General Contractors, Architects, and Owners to exceed
expectations.

Pillar Exterior Panels
Pillar knows Exterior Panels!
The Pillar Exterior Panel (EP), is a factory-fabricated exterior wall assembly comprised of the
cold-formed metal-framing, exterior sheathing, and air-vapor barrier (AVB). It requires no
changes to the project's specifications, and can be assembled utilizing any of the well known
brands in the industry.
As a factory-assembled solution, over 99% of the waste associated with the Pillar EP is handled off-site.
Our clients are reporting a reduction in the total cost of job-site waste removal of up to 10% based upon
the utilization of the Pillar EP.
Select Projects Gallery
Exterior Panel Projects List
Why Pillar Exterior Panels?
Quality
By performing the fabrication and assembly of the exterior wall structure in our off-site facility, we
are able to maintain a consistent level of quality that cannot be replicated by stick-building on site.
Schedule
Our process allows for (in most cases) a floor to be weathertight within two (2) weeks of the removal of concrete formwork.
Price
The installed cost of the Pillar EP is no greater than the installed cost of traditional "stick-built" construction
and in many cases can cost significantly less.
Better
On-Site, conventional assembly, oftentimes performed from a scaffolding many stories in the air, cannot achieve the consistency
and quality that our controlled manufacturing delivers.
Faster
Dried-in floor levels in as little as two (2) weeks. [CaseStudy supported] Project Critical Path savings from one (1) month
to upwards of two (2) months.
Safer
Ninety percent (90%) of the most dangerous near slabedge labor hours are removed and replaced with fabrication facility labor hours.

About Pillar Construction
We are a full-service specialty contractor with offices located in Washington DC, Texas, and Florida, and
are able to perform work across most of the Mid-Atlantic, the Northeast, and the Southern states.
Founded in 1995, the company has experienced rapid growth by delivering
on our promises to our clients. We self-perform a variety of scopes of work including Drywall, Rainscreen,
Restoration, EIFS, Stucco, Rough Carpentry, and Vapor / Air Barriers.
Our team of architects, engineers,
construction professionals and applicators’ combined experience qualify us to deliver excellent quality. We
believe in partnering with our clients throughout the construction process by getting involved in the early design
stages, providing value engineering options to achieve the best results at a competitive price, developing a
plan to complete the work, and then executing the plan safely.
