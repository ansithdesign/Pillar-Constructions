Pillar — a specialty contractor based in Alexandria.

Founded in 1995, Pillar is driven by a deep respect for craft and the rhythm of urban building. We focus on interiors and envelopes that enhance city landscapes, combining simplicity, functionality and a durable finish.

Guided by Raja Khoury and Mike Chalabi, the team works closely with owners, developers and general contractors to shape the buildings people live and work in.
